# Coatly Painting

[Coatly Painting](https://coatly.ca) provides professional interior and exterior painting services across Calgary, Cochrane, Okotoks, Airdrie, and surrounding Alberta communities.

Test colours before you paint with the [Coatly Paint Colour Visualizer](https://coatly.ca/visualizer) — preview real shades from leading paint brands on your own walls and exterior surfaces.

## Services

- **Residential & Commercial Painting:** Interior living spaces, trim, ceilings, and durable exterior coatings.
- **Cabinet & Door Refinishing:** Smooth factory-grade spray finishes for kitchen cabinets and garage doors.
- **Exterior Staining:** Weather-resistant deck and fence protection engineered for Alberta's climate.
- **Colour Consultation & Visualisation:** Digital wall previews and upfront project estimates.
